Monuments of India

Taj Mahal, Agra

  • Location: Agra, Uttar Pradesh
  • Built: 1632-1653; 21 years
  • Built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan

Red Fort, Delhi

  • Location: New Delhi
  • Built: 12 May 1639-6 April 1648; 9 years
  • Built for Mughal Empire

Gateway of India, Mumbai

  • Location: Mumbai, Maharashtra
  • Built: 1913-1924; 11 years
  • Built to commemorate the visit of King George V

Qutub Minar, Delhi

  • Location: Mehrauli, New Delhi
  • Built: 1199-1220
  • Built by Qutb ud-Din Aibak and Iltutmish

Lotus Temple, Delhi

  • Location: Kalkaji, New Delhi
  • Built: 1980-1986; 6 years
  • Bahá'í House of Worship

Stone Chariot, Hampi

  • Location: Hampi, Karnataka
  • Built: 16th century
  • Built for Vijayanagara Empire
